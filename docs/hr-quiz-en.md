# HR Policies Quiz

Source: `docs/hr-no-table.docx` (Employee Handbook - Attarat Group / AMCO)

## Questions

1. What is the maximum number of working hours allowed per week?
2. What is the daily grace period allowed for lateness without a deduction?
3. What is the maximum daily Early-Out duration using hourly leave, and how much does it become during Ramadan?
4. Within how many hours must a medical report be submitted for sick leave?
5. What is the overtime pay multiplier for regular weekdays?
6. What is the overtime pay multiplier for weekends or public holidays?
7. What is the payroll cut-off date for submitting overtime claims to be included in the same month's payroll?
8. What percentage of an employee's basic salary is deducted for their Social Security contribution?
9. What percentage does the company (employer) contribute to Social Security?
10. When is the 13th salary paid, and what condition is required to receive it in full?
11. What is the Annual Aggregate Limit of health insurance coverage for Class A?
12. How many outpatient visits per member per year are covered under health insurance?
13. What is the waiting period required before coverage applies to Tonsils & Adenoids, Deviated septum, Sinusitis, and Hernias?
14. What is the minimum number of candidates the Line Manager must shortlist for interviews in the recruitment process?
15. Within how many working days must a transportation reimbursement claim be submitted with supporting receipts?

---

## Reference Answers

1. **48 hours per week** maximum (Office hours 09:00–18:00, Site hours 08:00–17:00, 12-hour shifts, with reduced hours during Ramadan).
2. **Up to 10 minutes** of daily grace; any lateness beyond that requires justification or a leave deduction.
3. **Up to 4 hours per day**, reduced to **3 hours during Ramadan**, via the hourly leave system.
4. **Within 24 hours** of the absence, the medical report must be submitted.
5. **1.25x** the basic hourly rate on regular weekdays.
6. **1.50x** the basic hourly rate on weekends or public holidays.
7. **The 15th of each month**; claims submitted after this date roll over to the next month's payroll.
8. **7.5%** of the basic salary is deducted from the employee and paid to Social Security.
9. **14.25%** for non-hazardous occupations and **15.25%** for hazardous occupations, borne by the employer.
10. Paid in **June**; paid in full to employees with **12 months of service**, and prorated for those with less.
11. **JOD 200,000** per year for Class A.
12. **14 visits** per member per year.
13. **6 months** waiting period for these cases (Tonsils & Adenoids, Deviated septum, Sinusitis, Hernias).
14. A **minimum of five (5) candidates** must be shortlisted by the Line Manager for interviews.
15. **Within 5 working days** of the travel date, the claim must be submitted with receipts and justification.
